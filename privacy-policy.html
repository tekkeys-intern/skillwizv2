Summary of Our Student Data Pledge

SkillWiz is designed exclusively for educational capability building and career enablement. We do not sell student data, we do not monetize student records for targeted third-party advertising, and students retain full ownership of their verifiable Karm-Patri™ records.

1. Introduction & Scope

myPerfectice Educare Pvt. Ltd. ("SkillWiz," "we," "us," or "our") operates the SkillWiz 2.0 platform, accessible at www.myskillwiz.com and associated university-specific subdomains and mobile applications. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, process, and protect personal and institutional data when universities, colleges, students, faculty, and recruiters interact with our services.

By accessing or using SkillWiz, whether through an institutional agreement entered into by your university or as an individual authorised user, you acknowledge the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Data Fiduciary & Processor Roles

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India) and comparable international privacy legislation:

  • Your University / Higher Education Institution acts as the primary Data Fiduciary (or Data Controller), determining which student cohorts are onboarded and setting the academic curriculum parameters.
  • SkillWiz (myPerfectice Educare Pvt. Ltd.) acts as a Data Processor executing capability assessments, learning orchestration, examination management, and student portfolio verification on behalf of the institution. For direct platform accounts or verified alumni credentials, SkillWiz may also act as a Data Fiduciary for the maintenance of persistent Karm-Patri™ capability records.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only data necessary for verifiable educational outcomes, divided into four categories:

Category Data Elements Purpose
Identity & Academic Full name, institutional email address, student enrollment/roll number, degree program, branch/stream, graduation year. Account provisioning, role assignment, and cohort segmentation.
Assessment & Capability Baseline 4C evaluation scores, code submissions, subject quizzes, automated interview recordings, skill-gap heatmaps. Personalised AI learning pathways, diagnostic analytics, and capability benchmarking.
Karm-Patri™ Record Verified badges, industry micro-credentials, capstone project proofs, Day-0 productivity indices. Creating the permanent, tamper-resistant student capability record for campus placement and entrepreneurship.
Technical & Audit IP address, browser type, device telemetry, login timestamps, proctoring integrity logs. System security, examination anti-cheating audit trails, and platform performance monitoring.

4. How We Use Data

SkillWiz processes your data strictly for legitimate educational, academic transformation, and career enablement purposes, including:

  1. Adaptive Learning: Generating individual learning journeys and curating micro-practice modules based on measured starting points.
  2. Continuous Evidence Generation: Transforming assignments, coding challenges, and mock interviews into verifiable competence metrics.
  3. Institutional Dashboards: Providing Vice-Chancellors, Heads of Department, and Placement Teams with aggregate cohort readiness analytics and accreditation-ready reports (e.g. NAAC, NIRF, NBA).
  4. The Verified Pool (Recruiter Connect): Enabling students who explicitly choose to publish their Karm-Patri™ profile to be discoverable by vetted corporate hiring partners for job opportunities aligned to their verified capability.

5. AI Scoring & Decision-Making

SkillWiz incorporates machine learning and generative AI models (such as AI personal interview simulators and coding evaluators). In our platform:

  • AI provides diagnostic recommendations, skill-gap analysis, and simulated interview feedback.
  • AI scores do not automatically disqualify any student from institutional examinations or academic progression; human faculty and university administrators retain ultimate academic oversight.
  • Our algorithmic models are continuously monitored to prevent demographic or gender bias in score normalization across diverse university cohorts.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not rent, sell, or trade student or faculty personal data. We disclose data only in the following controlled scenarios:

  • Your University: Authorised faculty, mentors, and training & placement officers have role-based visibility into student progress.
  • Prospective Employers (Verified Pool): Profile data is only shared with recruiters when a student opts in or applies to an active campus opportunity.
  • Infrastructure Service Providers: Vetted cloud infrastructure, database hosting, and transactional communication providers bound by strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: When required by applicable Indian law, court order, or government directive.

7. Security & Data Residency

We maintain industry-standard physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards:

  • Encryption: All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3; data at rest is protected using AES-256 encryption.
  • Data Residency: Platform data for Indian universities is hosted in secure, certified cloud data centres located within the sovereign territory of the Republic of India.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Access to personal records is restricted on a strict need-to-know basis with multi-factor authentication for administrative staff.

8. Retention & Deletion

Student records are retained for the duration of the student's active enrollment at the university plus the active placement cycle (typically up to 12 months post-graduation), unless a longer retention period is mandated by university academic regulations or the student elects to maintain a persistent lifelong Karm-Patri™ alumni credential.

Institutions may request deletion or anonymization of cohort data upon contract conclusion, subject to statutory audit requirements.

9. User Rights & Redressal

Under the DPDP Act 2023, data principals (students, faculty, and users) have the right to:

  • Access a summary of personal data processed by SkillWiz.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete academic profile information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing was based on voluntary opt-in (e.g. Verified Pool recruiter discovery).
  • Seek grievance redressal regarding any aspect of personal data handling.
