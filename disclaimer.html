Essential Notice

SkillWiz is an AI-powered capability orchestration and assessment platform. We partner with universities to enhance student learning, baseline competencies, and employer readiness. SkillWiz is not a degree-granting university or a recruitment agency guaranteeing employment.

1. Educational Transformation Scope

SkillWiz 2.0 (developed and operated by myPerfectice Educare Pvt. Ltd.) provides digital infrastructure, AI learning paths, examination management, and capability analytics to higher education institutions. Our services supplement and enhance the formal academic curriculum provided by accredited universities and colleges.

SkillWiz does not award academic degrees, diplomas, or statutory academic credits in its own capacity. Formal academic recognition and degree conferral remain under the sole statutory authority of the client university or affiliating body.

2. Employability & Placement Outcomes

While SkillWiz equips universities with diagnostic frameworks, Day-0 productivity curricula, mock readiness engines, and the corporate "Verified Pool":

  • SkillWiz does not guarantee job offers, minimum compensation packages, or interview shortlisting for any individual student.
  • Final hiring decisions, interview schedules, background checks, and employment offers are made at the sole discretion of the hiring corporate entities.
  • Metrics such as "Day-0 Productivity Score" reflect demonstrated student competence on practical domain exercises and simulations and do not guarantee employee workplace performance under independent company management.

3. Entrepreneurship Track (EUTP) Notice

Our Entrepreneurship & University Transformation Program (EUTP) supports student founders through incubation frameworks, business modeling tools, and investor showcase events:

  • Platform content, mentoring sessions, and incubator playbooks are provided for educational and business development purposes only.
  • Nothing on SkillWiz constitutes financial, investment, legal, or securities advice.
  • SkillWiz does not guarantee funding, seed grants, venture capital investment, or commercial profitability for student-led startups.

4. AI Diagnostic Tools & Assessment Scoring

SkillWiz employs artificial intelligence models for real-time coding review, speech and sentiment feedback in mock interviews, and skill-gap diagnostic mapping:

  • AI feedback is inherently probabilistic and intended to serve as formative guidance to accelerate student improvement.
  • SkillWiz continually tests and refines AI models for fairness and domain accuracy, but does not warrant that AI diagnostics will be free of occasional variance.
  • Universities and students should consider AI scores as supplemental diagnostics alongside faculty mentorship and rigorous academic evaluation.

5. Industry Radar & Market Projections

The "Industry Radar" feature synthesizes macro-economic indicators, public hiring reports, industry whitepapers, and corporate competency requirements across high-growth Indian and global sectors (e.g. Chip Design, Defence Tech, EV, Robotics, AI):

  • Sectoral insights, in-demand roles, and core competencies represent analytical estimates reflecting observed market patterns at the time of publication.
  • Industry demand and corporate hiring priorities fluctuate according to macroeconomic forces, technology evolutions, and corporate restructuring beyond SkillWiz's control.

6. Third-Party Trademarks & Partner Credentials

Any corporate marks, OEM logos, partner badges, software tool names, or academic institution names displayed on this website or within the platform (such as Python, Linux, ROS, Verilog, or international certification bodies) are the intellectual property of their respective trademark holders.

Reference to these marks or technology standards indicates educational training focus, curriculum alignment, or partner integrations and does not imply direct statutory endorsement unless explicitly documented.

7. Institutional Accreditation Support

SkillWiz provides cohort-level analytics, outcome tracking, and competency mapping dashboards designed to assist universities with documentation for accreditation bodies (such as NAAC, NIRF, NBA, and ABET). However, actual accreditation scores and ratings remain entirely within the sovereign assessment authority of the respective statutory accrediting agencies.
