Our Institutional Guarantee

1. Zero Data Brokerage: We never sell student lists, contact data, or assessment records to data brokers, ad networks, or loan providers.
2. Student Ownership: Students own their learning journey, code projects, and credentials.
3. Explainable AI: Every AI recommendation displays transparent reasoning for why a specific pathway was suggested.

1. The Student Data Pledge

At SkillWiz (an initiative of myPerfectice Educare Pvt. Ltd.), we recognize that universities are custodians of student trust. As institutions adopt AI-powered capability benchmarking and continuous assessment, the sanctity of student data must remain inviolable.

We treat all student academic records, coding submissions, video mock interviews, and assessment scores as sensitive educational assets governed strictly by institutional purpose.

2. Ethical AI Principles in Learning Orchestration

SkillWiz utilizes artificial intelligence to power personalized learning journeys, adaptive practice, mock interview simulation, and skill-gap identification. Our AI models are governed by four foundational principles:

  • Pedagogical Intent: AI models are trained exclusively on domain-aligned educational content, syllabi, coding benchmarks, and industry role competencies.
  • Algorithmic Transparency: Students are informed whenever an AI agent evaluates an attempt (e.g. an automated code review or mock interview speech analysis). The scoring breakdown is provided to the student so they can learn from each iteration.
  • No Black-Box Gatekeeping: SkillWiz AI never acts as a barrier preventing a student from taking institutional examinations or graduating. AI serves as a diagnostic coach, not a disciplinary arbiter.
  • Privacy by Design: Models operate on pseudonymized identifiers during analytical processing; raw student identities are separated from training feedback loops.

3. Fairness & Anti-Bias Testing

Higher education cohorts encompass diverse socio-economic, linguistic, and regional backgrounds. To ensure equitable treatment:

  • Speech & Interview Models: Our AI speech analysis for mock interviews focuses on domain vocabulary, conceptual coherence, problem-structuring, and clarity—actively calibrated to accommodate varied Indian regional accents without penalizing non-native English speakers.
  • Continuous Metric Validation: We conduct periodic disparate impact analysis across gender, academic branch, and demographic tiers to ensure baseline scoring standards remain objective and merit-based.

4. Human-in-the-Loop Oversight

SkillWiz maintains that AI should amplify faculty capability, not replace educator judgment. In every implementation:

  • Faculty and department heads can review, calibrate, or override automated assessment recommendations.
  • Final evaluation grades for credit-bearing academic courses remain under the exclusive jurisdiction of university faculty.
  • Students have the explicit option to request human faculty re-evaluation for disputed mock assessment ratings.

5. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Matrix

Access to student capability information is governed strictly by institutional role:

Role Access Scope What They CANNOT See
Student Full access to their own personalized journey, Karm-Patri™ record, attempt histories, and practice feedback. Identities or individual score data of peer students.
Faculty & Mentors Assigned batch/section skill heatmaps, practice completion rates, mentoring intervention priorities. Private job search activity or confidential recruiter applications.
Training & Placement (TPO) Cohort employability readiness scores, role matching, Day-0 productivity status across departments. Unauthorized personal communication between students and personal mentors.
University Leadership (VC / Dean) Institution-wide capability benchmarks, year-over-year transformation trends, NAAC/NBA accreditation data. Granular personal user credentials.
Verified Pool Recruiters Verified student skill tags, verified projects, and Day-0 productivity scores ONLY for students who opted in. Private academic discipline notes, non-consented personal contact details.

6. Verified Pool & Recruiter Consent

The SkillWiz "Verified Pool" bridges verified student capability with corporate hiring partners. Participation in the Verified Pool is governed by explicit consent:

  • A student’s Karm-Patri™ profile is only made visible to corporate recruiters when the student explicitly activates their "Ready for Opportunities" preference.
  • Students can toggle their profile visibility to public, institutional-only, or private at any time from their profile settings.
  • Recruiters accessing the pool must be vetted corporate partners bound by strict anti-poaching and candidate confidentiality agreements.

7. Karm-Patri™ Portability & Graduation

We believe students should carry the proof of their hard work throughout their professional lives:

  • Lifelong Credential: Upon graduation, students can claim their verified Karm-Patri™ alumni credential as a portable digital portfolio.
  • Cryptographic Verification: Badges, certifications, and project completions are verifiable via tamper-evident URLs or digital hashes.
  • Data Portability: Students may export their project repositories, test summaries, and verified milestone certificates in standard portable formats (PDF, JSON).

8. Independent Audits & Institutional Governance

SkillWiz undergoes annual third-party cybersecurity vulnerability assessments and penetration testing (VAPT). We furnish institutional compliance reports to university governance boards upon request.
