Zero Advertising Cookies

SkillWiz is an institutional education platform. We do not place third-party behavioral advertising cookies (such as Meta Pixel or cross-site commercial ad networks) on our platforms or sell cookie-derived profiles.

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your browser, maintain active login sessions, remember user preferences (such as high-contrast display or reduced motion settings), and monitor technical platform health.

In addition to traditional HTTP cookies, SkillWiz may also utilize HTML5 Local Storage and Session Storage to store UI state parameters (such as the active tab in the platform layers visual or carousel play status) locally on your device for enhanced performance.

2. How SkillWiz Uses Cookies

We classify the technologies used on our platforms into three functional categories:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for user authentication, session security, CSRF protection, and load balancing across institutional clusters. Disabling these will cause the platform to fail to function.
  • Preferences & Accessibility: Remember your choices, such as reduced motion preferences, dark/light theme adjustments, and language settings.
  • Performance & Diagnostics: Measure page load times, script execution errors, and server response metrics in an aggregated, non-identifying manner to ensure high platform uptime during concurrent university examination events.

3. Detailed Cookie Inventory

The following table lists the primary cookies and local storage tokens deployed on the SkillWiz platform:

Name / Key Type Duration Purpose
__sw_sess Strictly Necessary Session Maintains encrypted authenticated session state for student, faculty, or TPO logins.

4. Zero Cross-Site Ad Tracking

Unlike consumer social networks or commercial media sites, SkillWiz does NOT use:

  • Retargeting pixels or ad-network beacons.
  • Browser fingerprinting for non-essential tracking.
  • Data brokers or cross-site consumer analytics aggregators.

Any telemetry collected is strictly used internally to optimize server throughput, prevent unauthorized platform access, and maintain application stability during peak campus assessment cycles.

5. How to Manage and Disable Cookies

You can configure your web browser to reject cookies, alert you before receiving a cookie, or delete existing cookies. To manage your preferences, consult your browser's official instructions:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies / Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

Note: If you disable strictly necessary cookies, you may be unable to authenticate into your student or institutional portal, and proctored examination workflows may be unavailable.
